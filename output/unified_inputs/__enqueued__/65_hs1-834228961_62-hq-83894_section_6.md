Fellow Americans,

I am writing the same letter to all three Departments. On August lst,I wrote a letter, and mailed it on August 2nd, to the Air Force Intelligence telling my views of a flying saucer. In about four days the physicist Noel Scott gaue out a report of findings identical to my report. Now if I haue the credit for it.I haue reason to belieue my ideas haue been hi-jacked. It should be easy to find out.when did Mr. Scott start his erperiment?h Which office did my letter go through2 Was my letter placed on file?It had a return address. I haue much better working proof of my theory than Mr.Scott,which has been before me all along, until I hit upon the idea.

The solution should not haue been published. There is agreat deal more to it than that, which I can erplain. The idea should haue been worked out thoroughly and secretly. Rainmakers could probably do a great deal of harm with the answer. There is a possibility that it could mean an entirely different type of air warfare. The Air Force could possibly use it for defensiue warfare. It could lead to man-made flying saucers which the enemy could use against us.

the information should be kept secret.
