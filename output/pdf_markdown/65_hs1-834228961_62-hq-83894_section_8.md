In response to a letter directed by him to Mr. Robert Cutler,Special Assistant to President Dwight D. Eisenhower,reflecting that he "might have some information about the rocket in Texas,"WladyslawKrasuski,5457 Joseph Campau,Detroit,was interviewed November 7,1957,

KRASUSE

and furnished the following information:

## Born (b) (6)

Krasuski was brought from Poland as a Prisoner of War to Gut Alt Golssen,approximately 3O miles east of Berlin,G Germany in May,1942,where he remained until a few weeks after the Displaced Persons Camps at Kork,Strasburg,Offenburg,Milheim and Freiburg, Germany.He attended a radio technician school mill at Laurachbaden,Germany.He arrived in the United States at New York,May 2,195l,via the "S.S. General Stewart" as a Displaced Person, destined to the Reverend Father Petro Walkowiak,Hamtramck, Michigan; his alien registration number (b) (6)

Since May,l95l,he has been employed at the Gobel

Brewery,Detroit.

News report of mysterious vehicle in Texas causing engines to stall prompted him to communicate with the United States Government concerning a similar phenomenon observed by him in l944 in the area of Gut Alt Golssen.

According to Krasuski,during l944,month not recalled,

while enroute to work in a field a short distance north of Gut Alt Golssen, their tractor engine stalled on a road through a swamp area. No machinery or other vehicle was then visible although a noise was heard described as a high-pitched whine similar to that produced by a large electric generator.

<!-- image -->

An"Ss" guard appeared and talked briefly with the German driver of the tractor,who waited five to ten minutes， after which the noise stopped and the tractor engine was started normally. Approximately3 hours later in the same swamp area,but away from the road where the work crew was cutting "hay",he surreptitiously,because of the German in charge of the crew and "ss" guards in the otherwise deserted area,observed a circular enclosure approximately lo0 to 150 yards in diameter protected from viewers by a tarpaulintype wall approximately 5o feet high,from which a vehicle was observed to slowly rise vertically to a height sufficient to clear the wall and then to move slowly horizontally a short distance out of his view,which was obstructed by nearby trees.

This vehicle,observed from approximately 500 feet,was described as circular in shape,75 to 100 yards in diameter,and about l4 feet high,consisting of dark gray stationary top and bottom sections, five to six feet high. The approximate three foot middle section appeared to be a rapidly moving component producing a continuous blur similar to an aeroplane propeller,but extending the cirThe cumferance of the vehicle so far as could be observed. noise emanating from the vehicle was similar but of somewhat lower pitch than the noise previously heard. The engine of the tractor again stalled on this occasion and no effort was made by the German driver to start the engine until the noise stopped, after which the engine started normally.

Uninsulated metal, possibly copper, cables one and one-half inch to two inches in diameter, on and under the surface of the ground,in some places covered by water, were observed on this and previous occasions, apparently running between the enclosure and a small concrete column-like structure between the road and enclosure.

This area was not visited by Krasuski again until shortly after the end of World War II, when it was observed the cables had been removed and the previous locations of the concrete structure and the enclosure were covered by water. Krasuski stated he has not been in communication since 1945 with any of the work crew of 16 or l8 men, consisting of Russian,French and Polish Pows, However,of these,Krasuski was able to recall by name only FranciszekGrabowski,no address known,described as then about 5O years of age and presumed by Krasuski to have returned to Poland after l945.
